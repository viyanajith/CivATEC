🍎 Mac: first time opening

  1. Open ATEC.dmg and drag ATEC into Applications.
  2. Open the game once. macOS will say it "could be malware" and block it — that's what it says about every indie game without Apple's $99 badge, not a real problem.
  3. Go to System Settings → Privacy & Security, scroll to the bottom and click Open Anyway. Your Mac asks for your password (it stays on your Mac).
  4. That's it — from now on it opens normally, forever.

🪟 Windows

If a blue "Windows protected your PC" box appears, click More info → Run anyway. Same reason: no paid badge.

> what you'll do

Survive

Health, hunger and air. Hunt chickens and sheep, cook your food, craft a bed before the first night — and watch out for lions in the forest.

Craft & smelt

Wood and stone tools, a furnace, iron and copper ore, armor. A recipe book in the workbench shows you how.

Electricity

Silicon from sand, batteries, wires and solar panels. Watch the power flow as glowing dots — panels only work in the sun.

The drill

A gear, pipes and a drill head. Power it and it bores a 3×3 tunnel, block by block, dragging along everything glued to it.

Glue & machines

Tap tree resin, mix glue, stick blocks together. Hoppers and a mechanical chest catch what your drill digs up.

Your world

Every world comes from a seed: plains, forests, beaches and seas. Type one in or roll a random one.

> the road so far

  • Wood Age — logs, planks, the workbench, your first pickaxe
  • Stone Age — stone tools, the furnace, cooked food
  • Iron Age — iron ore, iron tools and armor
  • Electric Age — copper, silicon, batteries, solar panels and wire
  • Machine Age — glue, hoppers and the drill
  • Next · Space — rockets, Mars, and one day a Dyson Sphere
